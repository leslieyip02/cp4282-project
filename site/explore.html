UNDER THE HOOD

What changed inside the splats?

Colors identify kernels throughout. Compare curves within the same scene and seed. The distributions describe saved 3D primitives at the selected step; their vertical axes show the percentage of splats in each bin.

Splat count over training

Active splats · dots are saved snapshots every 2,000 steps

How to read it

A rise means densification added splats faster than pruning removed them; a fall means the reverse. A flat line at 200k means the capacity limit is active, not that training or image quality has converged. Compare it with PSNR to see whether structural changes coincide with quality changes.

Scale distribution

log₁₀ world-space geometric scale · selected step

How to read it

Leftward bins contain smaller splats; rightward bins contain larger ones. A left shift can mean finer geometry, while a right shift can mean broader coverage. Scale is the geometric mean of three 3D axes, so this plot hides elongation and screen-space size. Neither direction alone proves better detail.

Opacity distribution

Sigmoid-transformed opacity · selected step

How to read it

Mass near 0 means many individually faint splats; mass near 1 means many nearly opaque splats. Compare the shape and the “Opacity ≥ 0.5” share above across kernels. This is a count of 3D primitives, not their contribution to visible pixels: size, overlap, depth, and visibility also matter.

Validation PSNR

Mean of eight fixed validation-camera PSNR values · every 500 steps

How to read it

Higher is lower pixel error on these reserved views. The curve can dip as optimization, densification, and pruning change the model; the same eight cameras are used each time, so it is not jitter from resampling views. A dip alone does not identify its cause. Check the dB axis, which zooms to the plotted range, and compare the final held-out test scores on Results.

Reading the set together

The count curve has its own scale, and each distribution is normalized within its kernel. A kernel can have more small or opaque splats without producing a better image. Use the matched render above to inspect visible changes, then the paired test metrics on Results to judge final quality.
