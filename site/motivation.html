01 / WHY THIS PROJECT

Does the fade shape matter?

A 3D splat becomes an ellipse on screen. The kernel controls how its opacity fades from the center to the edge. That choice can change image detail, optimization gradients, and the amount of rasterization work. This project measures those effects inside one renderer.

THE EXPLORATION

How does the screen-space weight affect rendering?

Compare Gaussian, linear radial, and degree-2 polynomial weights under matched settings within each scene. Observe held-out reconstruction quality, convergence behavior, and rendering cost. The 3D ellipsoids, projection, color model, and front-to-back compositing stay the same.

WHY TRY THIS

A practical comparison of three fade shapes

This project explores 3D Gaussian splatting by changing the pixel-weight function in one renderer. Each variant uses the same projected geometry and training framework within a scene. The three weights share a half-height radius, making their tails and support boundaries easier to compare.

The goal is to see how quality and cost respond under the chosen constraints, not to claim a new rendering method or a universal best kernel. Different resolutions, splat caps, training schedules, or backgrounds could change the result.
